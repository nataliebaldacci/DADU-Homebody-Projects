Owner Occupancy Requirements

Understanding the residency requirement for Nashville DADUs

Owner Must Live On-Site BL2025-1007 Restrictive Covenant Required

The Owner Must Live On-Site

Nashville requires the property owner to reside in either the main house OR the DADU. You cannot rent out both units while living elsewhere.

Why Does This Rule Exist?

Neighborhood Character

Keeps properties owner-occupied to maintain residential feel

Property Standards

Owner presence encourages better maintenance and upkeep

Community Investment

Owners living on-site are more invested in the community

Enforced Through Restrictive Covenant

The owner occupancy requirement is recorded in a restrictive covenant that stays with the property deed. This ensures compliance even if the property is sold. Learn more about covenants

What About Short-Term Rentals?

STR Rules for DADUs

Important: Short-term rentals (Airbnb, VRBO, etc.) are NOT permitted in DADUs located in RS, RN, or RL zones.

Owner-Occupied STRP

If your property qualifies (not in RS/RN/RL), you may apply for an owner-occupied short-term rental permit. You must live on-site and provide proof of residence.

View Permit Types

Proof of Residence Required

Nashville requires documentation proving you live at the property, such as a driver's license, voter registration, or utility bills in your name.

Legal Reference

"The owner of the lot on which the accessory dwelling unit is located shall occupy either the principal building or the accessory dwelling unit as the owner's primary residence."
Metro Nashville BL2025-1007, Section 17.16.035
