DADU Design Standards

Metro Nashville design requirements and standards for Detached Accessory Dwelling Units

Size & Scale Standards

  • Maximum Floor Area700 SF
  • Maximum Height24 feet / 2 stories
  • Max Lot Coverage AdditionVaries by zone
  • Minimum Unit Size150 SF (efficiency)

Note: Floor area includes all habitable space. Covered porches, garages, and unfinished basements are excluded from the calculation.

Setback Requirements

  • Side Setback (RS5)3 feet
  • Side Setback (RS7.5+)5 feet
  • Rear Setback3 feet
  • From Primary Structure6 feet minimum

Exception: Setbacks may be reduced when DADU replaces an existing accessory structure in the same footprint.

Parking Standards

  • Required Spaces (DADU)0 additional
  • Primary Dwelling Spaces2 spaces (existing)
  • Tandem ParkingPermitted
  • Driveway Width10 ft minimum

Key Benefit: DADUs do not require additional parking spaces beyond what's required for the primary dwelling.

Design Compatibility

Required Design Elements:

  • Roof PitchMatch primary or 4:12 min
  • Exterior MaterialsCompatible with primary
  • Window StyleSimilar proportions
  • Color PaletteComplementary

Overlay Districts: Additional design standards may apply in Historic or Contextual overlays.
