📋 Overlay Districts & Special Plans

Not every parcel is eligible for a DADU by-right. But your property might qualify through a DADU Overlay, Urban Design Overlay (UDO), or Specific Plan (SP) with DADU development standards.

⚡ Conditional Eligibility Pathway
1
🏙️

Urban Services District

Parcels in the USD with qualifying zoning are eligible by-right.

By-Right
2
📍

DADU Design District

Parcels in GSD within a DADU Overlay (DDU) may qualify.

Conditional
3
🎨

Urban Design Overlay

UDOs with specific DADU development standards may permit DADUs.

Conditional
4
📐

Specific Plan

Some SPs include DADU development standards allowing construction.

Conditional

⚠️ How to Check Your Property

Use Nashville Parcel Viewer to see if your parcel is in an overlay district. The base zoning and any overlays will be listed under the Zoning tab.

  • DDU (DADU Design District): Check for "DDU" in the zoning description
  • UDO (Urban Design Overlay): Look for "UD" overlay codes (e.g., 2021UD-001)
  • SP (Specific Plan): Look for "SP" codes (e.g., 2022SP-030)

Contact Metro Planning to confirm if your overlay includes DADU development standards.
