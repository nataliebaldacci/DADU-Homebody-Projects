BL2025-1007: The Landmark Bill

Enacted
Bill Number BL2025-1007
Effective Date December 12, 2025
Sponsor Metropolitan Council of Nashville-Davidson County

Summary

BL2025-1007 dramatically expanded DADU eligibility across Nashville-Davidson County. For the first time, properties in R and RS residential zones can build detached accessory dwelling units by right within the Urban Services District (USD). Properties in the General Services District (GSD) may also qualify through overlay districts. The legislation opened 67,707 parcels to DADU construction, representing a historic shift in Nashville's approach to housing density and affordability.

What Changed

Before BL2025-1007
  • Limited to specific overlay districts
  • Required special permits and approvals
  • Restricted to small geographic areas
  • Complex application process
  • Fewer than 5,000 parcels eligible
After BL2025-1007
  • All R and RS zones eligible
  • By-right in USD (no special permit)
  • 67,707 parcels now eligible
  • Owner occupancy required (principal or DADU)
  • Streamlined permitting process

Size Requirements

Lots Under 10,000 SF

Max Living Area 700 SF
Max Footprint 750 SF

Lots 10,000 SF or More

Max Living Area 850 SF
Max Footprint 1,000 SF
Additional Rule: The DADU height cannot exceed the height of the principal structure on the lot.
