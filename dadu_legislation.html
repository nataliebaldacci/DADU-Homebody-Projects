DADU Legislation & Design Standards

Official Nashville Metro legislation documents governing Detached Accessory Dwelling Units, including overlay district design standards and zoning amendments.

Understanding DADU Eligibility Paths

Your property's DADU eligibility depends on its location and zoning. There are different design standards based on your eligibility path:

  • USD (Urban Services District):By-right construction in R/RS zones - follows standard DADU requirements
  • GSD (General Services District):Requires DADU Overlay approval - must follow overlay-specific design standards
  • Historic Districts:Additional MHZC review required - see historic overlay guidelines

DADU Overlay District Plans

Design standards for overlay-approved areas

What are DADU Overlay Districts?

Properties in the General Services District (GSD) require approval through a DADU Overlay to build. These plans establish specific design standards for approved overlay areas, including setbacks, height limits, and architectural requirements.
