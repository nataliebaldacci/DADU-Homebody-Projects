Key Insights

BL2025-1007 Impact

The December 2025 legislation expanded eligibility to 67,707 parcels in the Urban Services District, a 340% increase from the previous overlay system.

Hot Markets

East Nashville (37206) leads with 17% of all permits, followed by Sylvan Park and 12 South. These areas have highest demand and fastest appreciation.

Cost Optimization

Modular/prefab DADUs average $165/SF vs $210/SF for custom builds. Consider modular for projects prioritizing budget over customization.
