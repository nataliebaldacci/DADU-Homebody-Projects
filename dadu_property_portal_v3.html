⚖️ Legal Requirements
  • ✓
    Owner Occupancy Requirement
    Owner must occupy either the principal dwelling or DADU. Recorded covenant required before permit. Learn more →
  • ⚠
    Restrictive Covenants / HOA
    Check your deed and HOA rules for restrictions that may prohibit ADUs. Search Register of Deeds →
  • ✓
    Short-Term Rentals
    DADUs follow same STR rules as principal dwelling. Owner-occupied STRP NOT permitted in DADU on RS/RN/RL zoned lots.
  • ℹ
    Floodplain Status
    Check property for FEMA flood zone restrictions. May require elevation certificate.
  • ℹ
    Historic Overlay
    Properties in historic overlays may require additional MHZC design review.
  • ⚠
    Utility Connections
    Contact Metro Water, NES, and Piedmont Gas for service requirements. Call TN811 before digging.
