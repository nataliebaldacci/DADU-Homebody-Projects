Common DADU Permit Mistakes

Real rejection reasons from Nashville-Davidson County DADU permit applications. Learn from documented issues to avoid costly delays on your next project.

Nashville's DADU permitting process under BL2025-1007 has specific dimensional, zoning, and procedural requirements that are commonly misunderstood. Zoning District Ineligibility

Nashville Metro Code 17.16.030
Example: Application in MUN District Denied
Reason: Parcel zoned MUN (Mixed Use Neighborhood). DADUs are only permitted in AG, AR2a, RS, R, and RM zoning districts per BL2025-1007. The MUN district is a mixed-use zone that does not qualify for by-right DADU construction.
Zoning: MUN
Eligible Zones: AG, AR2a, RS, R, RM
Example: GSD Without Qualifying Overlay Denied
Reason: Parcel is within the General Services District (GSD) but lacks a qualifying overlay district. Parcels in GSD require a DADU-specific or UDO overlay to be eligible. Urban Services District (USD) parcels are by-right.
Service District: GSD
Overlay Required: Yes (none present)

Contractor Takeaway

Always verify the zoning district AND service district before submitting. Use the Eligibility Map to confirm status. GSD parcels need an overlay. If the parcel is not in AG, AR2a, RS, R, or RM, stop. It is not eligible. See Code & Legislation for the full BL2025-1007 zoning text.

Size and Square Footage Violations

Nashville Metro Code 17.16.030.G
Example: Living Area Exceeds Lot-Based Maximum Revision Required
Reason: Proposed DADU living area of 1,100 SF on a lot under 10,000 SF. Lots under 10,000 SF are limited to 850 SF maximum living area. The contractor submitted plans for a 2BR/1BA unit that exceeded the threshold by 250 SF.
Lot Size: 8,200 SF
Proposed DADU: 1,100 SF living
Maximum Allowed: 850 SF living
Overage: 250 SF
Example: Footprint Exceeds 50% of Principal Home Revision Required
Reason: DADU footprint of 1,200 SF proposed on a lot where the principal home footprint is 1,800 SF. Maximum footprint is 850 SF or 50% of the principal home footprint (900 SF), whichever is greater. The 1,200 SF footprint exceeds both thresholds. Absolute maximum footprint is 2,500 SF.
Principal Footprint: 1,800 SF
50% of Principal: 900 SF
Proposed Footprint: 1,200 SF
Maximum Allowed: 900 SF

Contractor Takeaway

Size rules have two layers: living area (850 SF if lot < 10K SF; 1,200 SF if lot ≥ 10K SF) and footprint (850 SF or 50% of principal home footprint, whichever is greater, up to 2,500 SF max). Use the Size Calculator to verify both dimensions before drawing plans. Full dimensional standards at General Requirements.

Setback Violations

Nashville Metro Code 17.16.030.E
Example: Rear Setback Encroachment Revision Required
Reason: DADU placement encroaches into the required rear setback. The proposed structure was 3 feet from the rear property line where the minimum rear setback for the zoning district requires 5 feet. DADU must be located behind the principal structure and meet all applicable setback requirements.
Proposed Rear Setback: 3 ft
Required Minimum: 5 ft
Location: Behind principal
Example: DADU Not Behind Principal Structure Denied
Reason: DADU proposed alongside the principal structure rather than behind it. BL2025-1007 requires the DADU to be located behind the principal structure. The site plan showed the DADU positioned to the side of the main house, extending into the front yard setback area.
Placement: Side of principal
Required: Behind principal

Contractor Takeaway

DADUs must be behind the principal structure and meet all zoning district setbacks. Use the Draw DADU on Parcel tool to visualize placement. Check for easements that may further restrict the buildable area. A survey is strongly recommended before submitting plans. See setback tables at General Requirements.

Procedural Deficiencies

Nashville Metro Code 17.16.030.H-J
Example: Missing Owner Occupancy Affidavit Revision Required
Reason: Application submitted without the required owner occupancy affidavit. BL2025-1007 requires the property owner to occupy either the principal structure or the DADU. The signed affidavit must be recorded with the Davidson County Register of Deeds.
Missing Document: Owner Occupancy Affidavit
Recording: Davidson County ROD
Owner Occupancy Requirements → Form Wizard →
Example: Historic Overlay Review Not Obtained Revision Required
Reason: Parcel is within a historic or conservation overlay district. DADU applications in overlay districts require additional design review and approval from the Metro Historic Zoning Commission (MHZC) before a building permit can be issued. Plans must demonstrate architectural compatibility.
Overlay: Historic Conservation
Required Review: MHZC approval
Design Standard: Architectural compatibility
Overlay Districts → Design Standards →

Contractor Takeaway

The most common procedural issue is historic overlay review (37.8% of flagged applications). Always check for overlay districts before submitting.
