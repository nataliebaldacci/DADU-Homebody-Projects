DADU Eligibility Flowchart

Follow Nashville.gov's official step-by-step process to determine if your property qualifies for a Detached Accessory Dwelling Unit under BL2025-1007.

BL2025-1007 Nashville.gov Process 67,707 Eligible Parcels

How Eligibility Works

Nashville uses a four-step process to determine DADU eligibility. Your property must pass each step to qualify. If eligible, zoning conditions in Step 5 apply to your build.

1

Land Use Table Check

Does the Land Use Table show your parcel's zoning district permits DADUs with conditions (PC)?
YES
Your zoning district permits DADUs. Proceed to Step 2 to verify lot size.
NO
Your zoning district does not permit DADUs. Your parcel is not eligible.
Land Use Tables → Check Your Zoning → Base Zoning Map → BL2025-1007 →
2

Minimum Lot Area

Does your lot meet the minimum lot area required for your zoning district?
YES
Your lot meets the minimum area. Proceed to Step 3 to check your service district.
NO
Your lot is below the minimum area for your zoning district. Your parcel is not eligible.
Bulk Regulations → Setback Requirements → Check Lot Size →
3

Service District / Overlay Check

Does your parcel meet ONE of the following service district or overlay requirements?
A In USD (Urban Services District): DADU is permitted by-right. No overlay needed.
B In DADU Overlay (GSD): Parcel is in the General Services District but within a DADU Overlay. Eligible with overlay conditions.
C In UDO with DADU standards: Parcel is in an Urban Design Overlay that includes DADU standards. Eligible with UDO conditions.
D In SP with DADU standards: Parcel is in a Specific Plan that includes DADU standards. Eligible with SP conditions.
NONE None of the above: Parcel is in GSD without a qualifying overlay, UDO, or SP. Not eligible.
USD Map (PDF) → USD/GSD Boundary → USD Charter → DADU Overlay Districts → DADU Overlay Ordinance → UDO → Specific Plan → Overlay Districts ArcGIS → Check Overlays →
4

Single Principal Structure

Is there exactly ONE single-family principal structure on the parcel?
YES
Your parcel has one principal structure. You are eligible to build a DADU. Proceed to Step 5 for zoning conditions.
NO
Parcels with zero or multiple principal structures do not qualify. Your parcel is not eligible.
DADU Definition → Check Building Count →
5

IF ELIGIBLE: Zoning Conditions

Eligible -- Review Conditions Below

Your DADU must comply with all conditions in Metro Code Section 17.16.030G. These requirements apply to every DADU regardless of service district or overlay.

  • Owner Occupancy: The property owner must occupy either the principal dwelling or the DADU as their primary residence.
  • Size (Living Area): Lot under 10,000 SF: maximum 700 SF of living area. Lot 10,000 SF or greater: maximum 850 SF of living area.
  • Footprint: Cannot exceed the principal structure footprint. No other accessory building on the parcel may exceed 200 SF.
  • Height: Cannot exceed the height of the principal structure. Eave height max 10 ft (1-story) or 17 ft (2-story). Ridge must be lower than primary ridge, max 27 ft.
  • Setbacks: Footprint 850 SF or less placed to rear: half the required side setback (minimum 3 ft), rear setback minimum 3 ft. Footprint over 850 SF: full setbacks apply.
  • Design: DADU must have similar style, materials, color, and roof form as the principal structure.
  • STR Prohibition: New DADUs on single-family (RS), residential neighborhood (RN), or residential limited (RL) lots cannot be operated as short-term rentals.
Full DADU Conditions → Accessory Buildings → STR Restrictions → Restrictive Covenants → Full Title 17 →
6

Special Cases: Additional Review Required

Historic Overlay District

If your property is in a Historic Overlay, your DADU project must be reviewed and approved by the Metropolitan Historic Zoning Commission (MHZC). Design guidelines for your specific district will apply, and approval can take additional time.

Specific Plan (SP) or Urban Design Overlay (UDO)

If your property is in an SP or UDO that includes DADU standards, you must obtain approval from the Planning Department. Each SP and UDO has unique standards that may differ from the base zoning conditions listed in Step 5.

Historic Districts → Historic DADUs → BL2011-900 → Planning Contact → Register of Deeds → Metro Stormwater → Tennessee 811 → Building Code Ch. 16 → Nashville.gov DADU Page →

Quick Eligibility Checklist

  • Zoning district permits DADUs (Land Use Table shows PC for your district)
  • Lot meets minimum area for your zoning district
  • Property is in the USD, a DADU Overlay (GSD), a UDO with DADU standards, or an SP with DADU standards
  • Only one single-family principal structure on the parcel
  • Owner will occupy either the principal dwelling or the DADU as primary residence
