Important Conditions

Your Property Appears Eligible

Your DADU must comply with all of the following conditions under Metro Code Section 17.16.030G:

  • Height: Your DADU cannot exceed the height of your principal structure. Max eave height: 10 ft (single story) or 17 ft (two story). Max roof ridge: 27 ft. Measured from average finished grade.
  • Owner Occupancy: One of the two dwellings (principal or DADU) must be owner-occupied as a primary residence.
  • Location: DADU must be in the rear buildable area, behind the principal structure.
  • Restrictive Covenant: A restrictive covenant must be recorded before a building permit can be issued.
  • No STR: Short-term rental (owner-occupied) is not permitted in a new DADU on single-family, RN, or RL zoned lots.
  • Design: DADU must match the style, materials, and color of the principal structure.
