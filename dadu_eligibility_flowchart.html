Is your property zoned R, RS, RM, OR, or ORI?

DADUs are only permitted in residential zoning districts. Check your parcel's zoning code.

2

Is your parcel in the Urban Services District (USD)?

Under BL2025-1007 (effective Dec 2025), parcels in the USD can build DADUs by-right without special overlay.

🗺️ Check USD Map 📜 BL2025-1007
▼
YES (USD)

✅ By-Right Eligible!

📐 See Size Limits
NO (GSD)

⚠️ Check for Overlay

3

Does your lot have exactly ONE single-family home?

DADUs can only be added to parcels with a single principal dwelling. Multi-family, duplexes, or vacant lots don't qualify.

🔍 Check My Property
▼
YES

✅ Structure Eligible

Your lot has a qualifying principal structure. Continue to requirements.

NO

❌ Not Eligible

Lots with 2+ structures, multi-family, or no principal dwelling don't qualify for DADUs.

What size DADU can I build?

Maximum size depends on your lot area. Check your lot size to determine limits.
