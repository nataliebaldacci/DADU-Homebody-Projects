Step 5: Zoning Conditions -- Reviewed
5

IF ELIGIBLE: Zoning Conditions

Eligible -- Review Conditions Below

Your DADU must comply with all conditions in Metro Code Section 17.16.030G.

  • Owner Occupancy: The property owner must occupy either the principal dwelling or the DADU as their primary residence.
  • Size (Living Area): Lot under 10,000 SF: maximum 700 SF. Lot 10,000 SF or greater: maximum 850 SF.
  • Footprint: Cannot exceed the principal structure footprint. No other accessory building may exceed 200 SF.
  • Height: Cannot exceed principal structure height. Eave max 10 ft (1-story) or 17 ft (2-story). Ridge must be lower than primary, max 27 ft.
  • Setbacks: Footprint 850 SF or less placed to rear: half side setback (min 3 ft), rear min 3 ft. Over 850 SF: full setbacks.
  • Design: Must have similar style, materials, color, and roof form as the principal structure.
  • STR Prohibition: New DADUs on RS, RN, or RL lots cannot be operated as short-term rentals.
Full DADU Conditions → Accessory Buildings → STR Restrictions → Restrictive Covenants → Full Title 17 →
Step 6: Special Cases -- Reviewed
6

Special Cases: Additional Review Required

Historic Overlay District

If your property is in a Historic Overlay, your DADU project must be reviewed and approved by the Metropolitan Historic Zoning Commission (MHZC).

Specific Plan (SP) or Urban Design Overlay (UDO)

If your property is in an SP or UDO that includes DADU standards, you must obtain approval from the Planning Department.

Quick Eligibility Checklist

  • Zoning district permits DADUs (Land Use Table shows PC for your district)
  • Lot meets minimum area for your zoning district
  • Property is in the USD, a DADU Overlay (GSD), a UDO with DADU standards, or an SP with DADU standards
  • Only one single-family principal structure on the parcel
  • Owner will occupy either the principal dwelling or the DADU as primary residence
