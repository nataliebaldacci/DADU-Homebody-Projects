Nashville DADU Requirements Overview

Complete eligibility rules, size limits, setbacks, design standards, and permit process under BL2025-1007. Effective December 12, 2025.

1. Eligibility

Land Use Table · 17.12

To build a DADU, your property must meet ALL of the following criteria:

  • Land Use Table must show the parcel's zoning district permits DADUs with conditions (PC)
  • Lot area must comply with minimum lot area for the zoning district (see bulk regulation tables in 17.12)
  • Parcel must meet one of these four paths:
    • a. Be in the Urban Services District (by-right)
    • b. Be within a DADU Overlay in the General Services District
    • c. Be within an Urban Design Overlay (UDO) with DADU development standards
    • d. Be within a Specific Plan (SP) with DADU development standards
  • Only one DADU per parcel
  • Only when one single-family principal structure is present
  • When two or more principal structures exist, DADU is NOT permitted

5. Use Restrictions

17.04.060
Subordinate Structure
DADU must be clearly subordinate in size, height, and purpose to the principal structure. (17.04.060)
Same Lot, Detached
Must be located on same lot as principal structure and detached from it. Can be independent structure, dwelling above garage, or attached to workshop/accessory structure.
Owner Occupancy
Same owner must own both DADU and principal structure. One of the two dwellings must be owner-occupied.
Parking
One off-street parking space required for the DADU.
No Short-Term Rentals
No STRPs in DADUs on single-family, RN, or RL zoned lots.
Restrictive Covenant
Covenant must be recorded with Davidson County Register of Deeds before permit issuance.

6. Design Standards

17.16.030G.8
Style Matching
Similar style, design, and material color as principal structure. (17.16.030G.8)
Roof Form
Similar roof form and pitch as the principal structure.
Dormers
Must relate to style/proportion of windows. Cannot cover more than 50% of roof. Must be set back minimum 2 ft from exterior wall.
Accessory Structures
No other accessory structure can exceed 200 SF when a DADU exists on the lot.

7. Historic Overlay, SP, and UDO Properties

Overlay Districts

Historic Overlay Properties

  • Setbacks, size, and design enforced by Historic Commission
  • Use and living space enforced by Zoning (17.16.030G)
  • Historic Preservation Permit required in addition to building permit
  • Contact Metropolitan Historic Zoning Commission for design guidelines

Specific Plan (SP) or Urban Design Overlay (UDO)

  • Planning Department determines requirements
  • Building permit required with Metro Codes and Planning approval
  • SP or UDO development standards may impose additional restrictions beyond base zoning

8. Permit Process

5 Steps
1
Hire a Licensed Contractor
Property owners CANNOT pull self-permits for DADU construction or garage-to-DADU conversion. Homeowners can only self-permit renovations to an already-permitted DADU up to $25,000.
2
Submit to Zoning Help Desk
Contractor submits: residential permit application, site plan with distances to property lines/structures/easements, elevations from four corners, floor plan with fixtures/appliances.
3
Zoning Examiner Review
Application assigned to a zoning examiner for review against all DADU requirements.
4
Complete Signoff Checklist
Obtain all required signoffs from Codes divisions and Metro agencies before permit issuance.
5
Pay Fees and Build
Pay permit fees and begin construction. Schedule inspections with Metro Nashville throughout the build process.
Self-Permit Exception
Homeowners may only self-permit renovations to an already-permitted DADU, and only up to $25,000 in value. All new DADU construction and garage conversions require a licensed contractor.
