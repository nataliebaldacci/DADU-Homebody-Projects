Eligibility Rules

Nashville BL2025-1007 (effective December 12, 2025)

Zoning Requirement

Property must be zoned R or RS (Residential or Residential Single-Family).

Urban Services District

Properties in the USD can build by right. GSD properties need a DADU overlay district.

Lot Under 10,000 SF

Maximum 700 SF living area and 750 SF footprint.

Lot 10,000 SF or More

Maximum 850 SF living area and 1,000 SF footprint.

Height Limit

DADU cannot exceed the height of the principal structure.

Owner Occupancy

Required. Owner must live in the principal dwelling or the DADU.

Key Terms

Urban Services District (USD)The urban core of Nashville with full city services. 67,707 parcels eligible by right.
General Services District (GSD)Suburban areas outside the USD. Requires a DADU overlay district or eligible UDO.
DADU Overlay DistrictSpecial zoning overlay that permits DADUs in GSD areas where base zoning would otherwise restrict them.
Restrictive CovenantA recorded document that may restrict what you can build. Does not override zoning but may add private restrictions.

Source: Metro Nashville BL2025-1007
