What It Does

The DADU Eligibility Map is your first stop for understanding your property's potential.

Visual Eligibility Status

Color-coded parcels show eligible (green), conditional (yellow), and not eligible (red) at a glance.

Zoning Analysis

Automatically checks RS, R, and other residential zones against DADU allowance rules.

Click for Details

Click any parcel to see lot size, zoning code, and a link to the full Property Report Card.

How It Works

Three simple steps to check your property

1

Search or Navigate

Enter an address in the search bar or navigate the map to find your property. The map loads all 180,000+ Davidson County parcels with eligibility data.

2

View Eligibility Color

Parcels are color-coded: green for eligible, yellow for conditional (may need variance), and red for not eligible based on zoning or lot constraints.

3

Get Your Full Report

Click any parcel to see quick details, then launch directly into the Property Report Card for the complete eligibility analysis with documents, setbacks, and more.
