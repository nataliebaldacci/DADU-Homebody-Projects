Size Limits (BL2025-1007)

▼
✓ Requirement Your Lot
Lot < 10,000 SF → Max living space: 850 SF Check →
Lot ≥ 10,000 SF → Max living space: 1,200 SF
DADU cannot exceed size of principal structure
DADU footprint ≤ footprint of principal structure
Footprint Limits
  • Lot < 10,000 SF: Max footprint 750 SF
  • Lot ≥ 10,000 SF: Max footprint 1,000 SF
  • If DADU exists, no other accessory structure > 200 SF
5

Height Limits

▼
✓ Requirement
DADU height ≤ principal structure eave line
Single-story: Max eave height = 10 ft (from avg finished grade)
Two-story: Max eave height = 17 ft (from avg finished grade)
Roof ridge < principal structure ridge
Roof ridge ≤ 27 ft (from avg finished grade)
6

Setback Requirements

▼
✓ Requirement
DADU located behind (rear of) principal structure
Not over easement or platted setback
Not over side street setback (corner lots)
Setback Rules by Footprint Size
  • Footprint ≤ 850 SF:
    • Side setback = ½ of zoning requirement (min 3 ft)
    • Rear setback = min 3 ft
    • If garage faces alley: rear setback = 10 ft
  • Footprint > 850 SF: Full zoning district setbacks apply
7
