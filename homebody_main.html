What is a DADU?

A Detached Accessory Dwelling Unit (DADU) is a secondary residence built on the same lot as your primary home. Nashville's new legislation (BL2025-1007) allows DADUs by-right in the Urban Services District, opening up housing options for over 160,000 parcels.

Whether you want to house family members, generate rental income, or add property value, understanding the regulations is the first step.
