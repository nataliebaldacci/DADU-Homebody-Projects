Tennessee Clean Sites Fund - Stakeholder & Funding Flow

How DCERP + BRAG + Insurance Archeology Stack Together

PHASE 1: ACQUISITION (Months 1-3)
Month 1
🏠 Trapped Property Owner
Family inherited contaminated dry cleaner site. Can't sell (liability), can't finance (banks require Phase I), can't develop (permits trigger compliance).
↓
💼 Tennessee Clean Sites Fund
Acquires property at brownfield discount (30-50% below market). Takes on environmental liability.
PHASE 2: REGULATORY ENROLLMENT (Months 2-4)
Month 2-3
📋 VOAP Enrollment (Required)
Voluntary Cleanup Oversight and Assistance Program
• Entry Fee: $3,000/site
• Site Characterization: $2,500/site
• Annual Fee: $4,000/site
Required for both DCERP and BRAG funding
⚠️ Key Requirement: Must enroll in VOAP within 30 days of any BRAG contract execution
PHASE 3: FUNDING STACK (Months 3-12)
Months 3-6
🏛️ DCERP (Primary)
Dry Cleaner Environmental Response Program
• Covers: 90% of remediation costs
• Deductible: $50,000
• Eligible: Property owners (as of Jan 2026)
• Timeline: Ongoing during remediation
🏗️ BRAG (Supplemental)
Brownfield Redevelopment Area Grant
• Assessment: Up to $100K (24 months)
• Remediation: Up to $500K (24 months)
• Requires: Municipal partner to apply
• DCERP sites: Case-by-case eligibility
🔍 Insurance Archeology
Pre-1986 CGL Policy Recovery
• Potential Recovery: $50K-$200K
• Legal Fees: $15K-$30K upfront
• Success Rate: ~60-70% if policies exist
• Timeline: 6-18 months
💰 Tax Credits
F&E Tax Credits (SB0271)
• Tier 1/2: 50% of purchase price
• Tier 3/4: 75% of purchase price
• OR up to $500K based on remediation costs
• 25-year carry forward
PHASE 4: REMEDIATION (Months 6-24)
Months 6-24
🧪 Environmental Consultant
• Phase I ESA: $3,000-$5,000
• Phase II ESA: $15,000-$50,000
• Remediation Plan & Execution: $100K-$400K
• Goal: NFA (No Further Action) or NLUR (No Land Use Restriction) letter from TDEC
PHASE 5: EXIT (Months 24-36)
Months 24-36
🏗️ Exit to Developer
• LIHTC Developer (affordable housing)
• Market-rate Developer
• Mixed-use Developer
Sale Price: Clean land at market value (2-3x acquisition cost)
