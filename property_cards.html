What's on a Property Card?

  • Assessed Value— Land and improvement values used for tax calculation
  • Building Details— Square footage, year built, construction type, bedrooms/baths
  • Land Info— Lot size, acreage, land use code, zoning classification
  • Sales History— Recent transactions with sale price and date
  • Tax District— USD/GSD classification and applicable tax rate
  • Aerial Photo— Patriot Properties aerial imagery of the parcel
