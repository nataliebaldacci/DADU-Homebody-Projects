Contractor Checklist: Things You Need to Know

Beyond the standard zoning and bulk standards, these are the non-obvious requirements that cause delays and denials. Miss any one and you could lose weeks.

1

DADU Cannot Be in the Floodplain

If any part of the buildable area is in a FEMA floodplain or floodway, the DADU is not permitted there. Check the flood zone before designing. See Requirements

2

Restrictive Covenant Must Be Filed at Permit Time

A restrictive covenant must be recorded with the Davidson County Register of Deeds BEFORE the building permit is issued. This takes 2-3 weeks. Start early. Check Covenants

3

Owner Occupancy Affidavit Is a Blocking Requirement

The owner must sign and record an affidavit committing to live in either the principal home or the DADU. No affidavit = no permit. See Details

4

Self-Permits Are NOT Allowed for DADUs

You cannot use the expedited self-permit process. DADUs require full permitting: zoning help desk, building permit, then trade permits. See Code

5

Historic Overlay Requires Separate MHZC Approval

Properties in historic or conservation overlays need approval from the Metro Historic Zoning Commission BEFORE the building permit. MHZC meets the 3rd Wednesday of each month. Check Overlays

6

Easements Can Kill Your Design

Utility, drainage, and stormwater easements reduce the buildable area. You cannot build over an easement. Get a survey before you draw plans. See Requirements

7

No Other Accessory Structure Over 200 SF

If you build a DADU, no other accessory structure on the lot (garage, shed, workshop) can exceed 200 SF. This surprises homeowners with large detached garages. See Code

8

Water/Sewer Tap Fees Are Separate

Metro Water Services charges separate tap and capacity fees for new connections. These are NOT included in the building permit fee and can be significant. See Trade Permits

9

Design Must Match the Principal Home

DADUs must have similar style, materials, color, and roof form as the principal structure. This is legally enforced, not advisory. Incompatible designs get denied. Design Standards
