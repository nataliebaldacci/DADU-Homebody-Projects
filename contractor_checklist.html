Phase 1: Site Evaluation
Verify the parcel qualifies before you invest time
0/8
Confirm zoning district is eligible
Parcel must be zoned AG, AR2a, RS, R, or RM. No other districts qualify under BL2025-1007.
Check Eligibility Map
Required ~5 min
Verify service district (USD vs. GSD)
USD parcels are by-right. GSD parcels require a qualifying DADU or UDO overlay district. No overlay = not eligible.
See Code & Legislation
Required
Check for overlay districts (historic, conservation, design)
Historic and conservation overlays require separate MHZC approval BEFORE building permit. MHZC meets 3rd Wednesday of each month. Add 4-6 weeks to timeline.
Check Overlay Districts
Required Common Gotcha
Confirm parcel is NOT in a floodplain
DADUs cannot be built in FEMA floodplains or floodways. Check the FEMA flood zone map for the parcel before proceeding.
See Requirements
Required Common Gotcha
Verify existing single-family home on lot
A principal single-family dwelling must already exist. Cannot build DADU on vacant lot or simultaneously with principal structure.
Required
Search for restrictive covenants
Over 43,000 Nashville parcels have recorded covenants. Some prohibit secondary structures entirely. Check before investing in design.
Search Covenants Database
Required ~15 min
Identify easements on the parcel
Utility, drainage, and stormwater easements reduce buildable area. You cannot build over an easement. Get a survey or check the recorded plat.
Required Common Gotcha
Calculate maximum DADU size
Living area: 850 SF max (lot < 10K SF) or 1,200 SF max (lot ≥ 10K SF). Footprint: 850 SF or 50% of principal home, whichever is greater, up to 2,500 SF.
Use Size Calculator
Required ~5 min
Phase 2: Pre-Permit Preparation
Documents and filings required before submitting
0/8
Commission a property survey
Hire a licensed surveyor to verify property boundaries, locate easements, and identify buildable area. Required for permit application.
Required $400-800 1-2 weeks
File restrictive covenant with Register of Deeds
A restrictive covenant must be recorded BEFORE the building permit is issued. This takes 2-3 weeks to process. Start early. The covenant restricts subdivision and ensures the DADU remains accessory.
Download Forms
Required Common Gotcha 2-3 weeks
Obtain owner occupancy affidavit
Property owner must sign an affidavit committing to occupy either the principal home or the DADU. Must be recorded with Davidson County ROD. No affidavit = no permit.
Owner Occupancy Details
Required Common Gotcha
Prepare site plan with measured setbacks
Show DADU location behind principal structure, distances to all property lines, existing structures, and easements. DADU must be to the rear of principal.
Draw DADU on Parcel
Required
Create elevation drawings from four corners
Required for permit submission. Must show actual height measurements. DADU height cannot exceed the principal structure's height.
Design Standards
Required
Verify design compatibility with principal structure
DADU must have similar style, materials, color, and roof form as the principal home. This is legally enforced, not advisory. Incompatible designs get denied.
Design Standards

Check if other accessory structures exceed 200 SF
If building a DADU, no other accessory structure on the lot (garage, shed, workshop) can exceed 200 SF. Discuss with homeowner before proceeding.

Phase 3: Permitting
Submit applications and get approvals
0/7
If in historic overlay: submit MHZC application first
Properties in historic or conservation overlays need Metro Historic Zoning Commission approval BEFORE building permit. Submit "Outbuilding & DADU Application" to MHZC. Commission meets 3rd Wednesday.
Overlay Districts
If applicable 4-6 weeks
Submit building permit to Metro Codes (NOT self-permit)
Self-permits are NOT allowed for DADU construction. Must go through full permitting: zoning help desk review, then building permit application via ePermits. Include drawings, site plan, survey, affidavit.
Required Common Gotcha $1,500-4,000
Apply for water/sewer tap permits
Metro Water Services charges separate tap and capacity fees for new connections. These are NOT included in building permit fees and can be significant. Budget accordingly.
Trade Permit Details
Required Common Gotcha $2,000-5,000
Pull electrical, plumbing, and mechanical trade permits
Each trade requires a separate permit pulled by licensed sub-contractors. Include equipment specs, load calculations, and material lists.
Required Trade Permits

Phase 4: Construction
Build the DADU and pass inspections
0/6
Site preparation, grading, and foundation
Clear site, excavate, install footings, and pour foundation. Schedule and pass foundation inspection before framing.
1-2 weeks
Framing and rough-in (electrical, plumbing, HVAC)
Complete framing, install rough electrical, plumbing, and HVAC. Schedule framing inspection. If 2-story: stairs must be interior and enclosed.
3-4 weeks
Insulation inspection and drywall
Install insulation per energy code requirements. Pass insulation inspection before hanging drywall. Finish and tape drywall.
2-3 weeks
Interior finishes (flooring, cabinets, fixtures)
Install flooring, cabinets, countertops, plumbing fixtures, appliances, lighting, and trim work. Complete interior painting.
3-4 weeks
Exterior finishes (siding, roof, paint)
Complete siding, roofing, windows, doors, and exterior paint. Materials and colors must match principal structure for design compatibility.
2-3 weeks
Landscaping, driveway, and site restoration
Complete driveway (max 12 ft wide, max one curb-cut), walkways, landscaping, and final grading. Ensure drainage away from structures.
Driveway Requirements
1-2 weeks
Phase 5: Closeout & Handoff
Final inspections, CO, and client handoff
0/5
Pass all final inspections
Schedule and pass final building, electrical, plumbing, mechanical, and fire inspections. Address any corrections promptly.
Required
Obtain Certificate of Occupancy
Once all inspections pass, receive the CO from Metro Codes. DADU cannot be legally occupied without a Certificate of Occupancy.
Required
Remind owner: update insurance for DADU
Owner needs to add the DADU to their homeowner's insurance policy. If renting the DADU, landlord insurance may be required.
Required
Remind owner: STR restrictions apply
Short-term rental use of the DADU is prohibited in RS, RN, and RL zones even if owner-occupied. In other zones, STR requires owner occupancy. Inform the homeowner.
