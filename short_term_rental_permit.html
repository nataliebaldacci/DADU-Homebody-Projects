Short Term Rental Permits

Understanding STR Rules for Nashville DADUs

The Core Rule

In Nashville, you cannot operate a DADU as a short-term rental (STR) unless you meet the owner-occupancy requirement. The owner must reside on the property as their primary residence.

Important: STR Permit Phase-Out

Nashville has implemented restrictions on new non-owner-occupied STR permits in most residential zones. Many areas are no longer issuing new permits. Always verify current availability with Metro Codes before planning an STR investment.

STR Permit Types & DADU Eligibility

Owner-Occupied STR

May Be Eligible

If you live in the main house as your primary residence, you may be able to rent the DADU as a short-term rental. This is the only scenario where STR use of a DADU is potentially permitted.

Non-Owner-Occupied STR

Not Permitted

You cannot operate a DADU as a non-owner-occupied STR. The owner-occupancy requirement for DADUs means you must live on the property, making non-owner-occupied STR operation impossible.

Requirements for Owner-Occupied STR Permit

  • Primary Residence: You must live in the main house or the DADU as your primary residence (at least 51% of the year)
  • Valid DADU Permit: Your DADU must have received all required building permits and passed final inspection
  • Zoning Compliance: Property must be in a zone that permits owner-occupied STRs
  • No Restrictive Covenants: HOA or deed restrictions must not prohibit short-term rentals
  • Safety Requirements: Fire extinguishers, smoke detectors, CO detectors, and posted evacuation plan required
  • Parking: One off-street parking space per rental unit

Application Process

1

Complete DADU Construction

Ensure your DADU has received Certificate of Occupancy and all building permits are closed out.

2

Verify Zoning Eligibility

Contact Metro Codes to confirm your property is in a zone that allows owner-occupied STR permits.

3

Submit STR Application

Apply through Metro Nashville's online permit portal. Include proof of primary residence and property ownership.

4

Pass Safety Inspection

Schedule and pass the required safety inspection for fire and life safety compliance.

5

Receive Permit & Register

Once approved, display your permit number on all rental listings and register for hotel occupancy tax collection.

STR Permit Fees

Fee Type Amount
Initial Application Fee $313

Hotel Occupancy Tax 6% of rental income
State Sales Tax 7% of rental income
Local Option Tax 2.25% of rental income

Consider Long-Term Rental Instead

Given the restrictions on STR permits and the owner-occupancy requirement for DADUs, many homeowners find that long-term rental (leases of 30+ days) is a simpler option. Long-term rentals do not require an STR permit and can provide stable, consistent income without the complexity of STR regulations and tax collection.

Legal Reference

"Short term rental property means a residential dwelling unit containing not more than four (4) sleeping rooms that is used and/or advertised for rent for transient occupancy by guests..."

— Metro Nashville Code of Laws, Section 17.16.070

Information current as of 2024. Always verify with Metro Nashville Codes for the latest regulations.
