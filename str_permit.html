Short-Term Rental Permits

Understanding STR requirements for DADUs in Metro Nashville

⚠️

Important: Owner Occupancy Requirement

DADUs in Nashville require owner occupancy of either the primary dwelling OR the DADU itself. Short-term rentals of the DADU may only be permitted if you reside in the primary dwelling and obtain proper STR permits.

STR Permit Types

Owner-Occupied STR

Rent your DADU while living in the primary dwelling

Permit Type Type 1 - Owner Occupied
DADU Eligibility Potentially Allowed
Annual Permit Fee $313
Renewal Annual
Zoning Check Required

Non-Owner Occupied STR

Investment property short-term rental

Permit Type Type 2/3 - Not Owner Occupied
DADU Eligibility Not Allowed
Reason Owner occupancy required
Alternative Long-term rental only
Note DADU must be occupied by owner OR primary dwelling

STR Permit Requirements

📋 Application Documents

  • ✓ Completed STR application form
  • ✓ Proof of property ownership
  • ✓ Valid government ID
  • ✓ Floor plan of rental unit
  • ✓ Proof of insurance ($1M liability)

🏠 Property Requirements

  • ✓ Working smoke detectors
  • ✓ Carbon monoxide detectors
  • ✓ Fire extinguisher
  • ✓ Posted emergency info
  • ✓ Adequate parking

⚠️ Restrictions

  • ! Not in R/RS zones without owner occupancy
  • ! Distance requirements from other STRs
  • ! Maximum occupancy limits (2/bedroom + 2)
  • ! Quiet hours 10pm - 7am
  • ! No events or parties

Considering a DADU for Rental Income?

While STR permits for DADUs have restrictions, long-term rentals are permitted in all eligible zones. Many DADU owners find that long-term tenants provide more stable income with less management overhead.
