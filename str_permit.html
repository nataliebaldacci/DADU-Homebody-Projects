Short-Term Rental Permits

Understanding STR requirements, permit types, fees, and restrictions for DADUs in Metro Nashville-Davidson County.

Owner Occupancy Required Metro Code 17.16.070 BL2025-1007

The Core Rule

In Nashville, you cannot operate a DADU as a short-term rental unless you meet the owner-occupancy requirement. The owner must reside on the property as their primary residence.

STR Permit Phase-Out

Nashville has restricted new non-owner-occupied STR permits in most residential zones. Many areas are no longer issuing new permits. Always verify current availability with Metro Codes before planning an STR investment.

BL2025-1007 RESTRICTION

New DADUs on RS/RN/RL Lots: No STR Permitted

"Upon enactment of this section, on any single-family-zoned, Residential Neighborhood-zoned, or Residential Limited-zoned lot where a new DADU is built or established via conversion, short term rental property -- owner occupied is NOT a permitted use in the accessory dwelling unit."

This means new DADUs built under BL2025-1007 on RS, RN, or RL lots cannot have any STR at all -- not even owner-occupied. This restriction applies to DADUs built or converted after December 12, 2025.

Requirements for Owner-Occupied STR Permit

  • Primary Residence: You must live in the main house or the DADU as your primary residence (at least 51% of the year)
  • Valid DADU Permit: Your DADU must have received all required building permits and passed final inspection
  • Zoning Compliance: Property must be in a zone that permits owner-occupied STRs
  • No Restrictive Covenants: HOA or deed restrictions must not prohibit short-term rentals
  • Safety Requirements: Fire extinguishers, smoke detectors, CO detectors, and posted evacuation plan required
  • Parking: One off-street parking space per rental unit

Application Process

1

Complete DADU Construction

Ensure your DADU has received a Certificate of Occupancy and all building permits are closed out.

2

Verify Zoning Eligibility

Contact Metro Codes to confirm your property is in a zone that allows owner-occupied STR permits. Remember: new DADUs on RS/RN/RL lots are not eligible.

3

Submit STR Application

Apply through the Metro Nashville ePermits portal. Include proof of primary residence and property ownership.

4

Pass Safety Inspection

Schedule and pass the required safety inspection for fire and life safety compliance.

5

Receive Permit and Register

Once approved, display your permit number on all rental listings and register for hotel occupancy tax collection.

Legal Reference

"Short term rental property means a residential dwelling unit containing not more than four (4) sleeping rooms that is used and/or advertised for rent for transient occupancy by guests..."
Metro Nashville Code of Laws, Section 17.16.070

Consider Long-Term Rental Instead

Given the restrictions on STR permits and the owner-occupancy requirement for DADUs, many homeowners find that long-term rental (leases of 30+ days) is a simpler option. Long-term rentals do not require an STR permit and can provide stable, consistent income without the complexity of STR regulations and tax collection. Use the ROI Calculator to compare your options.
