Requirements for All DADUs

Owner Occupancy

Property owner must live in either the main house OR the DADU. You cannot rent out both units while living elsewhere.

Restrictive Covenant

A restrictive covenant must be recorded with the Davidson County Register of Deeds before your building permit can be issued.

Height & Size Subordination

DADU cannot be taller or larger than the principal structure. Max roof ridge is 27 ft but must be less than the main house.

No Short-Term Rentals

Short-term rental permits (Airbnb/VRBO) are NOT allowed for DADUs in RS, RN, or RL zoning districts.

Source: Metro Nashville BL2025-1007 • Metro Code § 17.16.030(G)
