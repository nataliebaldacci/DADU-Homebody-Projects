Search Methods

Multiple ways to find any Davidson County property

By Address

Enter the street address. We'll match it to the parcel and show you results. Example: 123 Main St Nashville

By APN (Parcel Number)

Enter the 11-digit Assessor Parcel Number for exact matching. Example: 11801012200

By Owner Name

Search by property owner name to find all their Davidson County properties. Example: Smith John
