What We Do

A single platform that brings together everything you need to evaluate, plan, and build a Detached Accessory Dwelling Unit in Nashville-Davidson County.

Homebody Projects aggregates public parcel data, building permit records, restrictive covenants, and regulatory information into one searchable platform. Instead of navigating dozens of government portals, property owners, contractors, and legal professionals can access everything from a single interface.

The platform covers all 285,512 parcels in Davidson County. For each property, we provide eligibility status, zoning classification, lot dimensions, building footprint data, permit history, covenant restrictions, and direct links to official Nashville government records.

Our data pipeline cross-references five independent ADU indicator sources (Regrid building counts, Metro permits, third-party permit records, secondary address indicators, and restrictive covenant filings) to achieve 98.9% detection accuracy against confirmed two-unit properties.

67,707 Parcels Eligible Under BL2025-1007

The BL2025-1007 Opportunity

Nashville's landmark DADU legislation, effective December 12, 2025, dramatically expanded where homeowners can build accessory dwelling units.

BL2025-1007 opened DADU construction across Nashville's residential zones. Properties zoned R or RS are now eligible, with by-right construction permitted inside the Urban Services District (USD). Properties in the General Services District (GSD) require an overlay district for eligibility. This single legislative change expanded DADU eligibility to 67,707 parcels across the county.

The legislation establishes clear size limits based on lot area, ensuring DADUs remain proportionate to their neighborhoods. Height cannot exceed the principal structure, and the property owner must occupy either the main home or the DADU.

Criterion Requirement
Eligible Zoning R or RS zones only
Urban Services District (USD) By-right construction
General Services District (GSD) Requires overlay district
Lot Under 10,000 SF Max 700 SF living area / 750 SF footprint
Lot 10,000 SF or Larger Max 850 SF living area / 1,000 SF footprint
Height Cannot exceed principal structure
Owner Occupancy Required (principal or DADU)
