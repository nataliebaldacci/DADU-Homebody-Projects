Historic Preservation Permits

Additional permits required for DADUs in Nashville's historic overlay districts. The Metro Historic Zoning Commission (MHZC) must approve your project before a building permit can be issued.

When Is It Required?

  • Property is in a Historic Preservation Zoning Overlay (HPZO) such as Edgefield, Germantown, Salemtown, or Lockeland Springs
  • Property is in a Neighborhood Conservation Zoning Overlay (NCZO) such as Waverly-Belmont, Richland-West End, or Eastwood
  • Property is an individually designated historic landmark
  • Check your overlay status at Nashville Parcel Viewer or the Eligibility Map

Design Standards

  • Must match the style, materials, and roof form of the principal structure
  • Windows and doors must complement the principal structure's proportions
  • Dormers permitted if they match the principal structure's window style
  • Corner lots are held to a higher standard (visible from public street)
  • Must follow district-specific design guidelines adopted by MHZC

Review Process

  • Record restrictive covenant with Register of Deeds first
  • Submit preservation permit application to MHZC
  • Staff review (approx. 4 business days) or full Commission review
  • MHZC meets 2:00 PM on the 3rd Wednesday of each month
  • Preservation permit valid for 6 months after approval
  • Then apply for Codes building permit separately

Dimensional Limits (Historic Overlay)

  • Maximum living space: 700 SF (all lots)
  • 1-story footprint: 700 SF or 50% of principal, whichever is less
  • 2-story footprint: 550 SF or 40% of principal, whichever is less
  • Max eave height: 10 ft (1-story) / 17 ft (2-story)
  • Min 20 ft separation from principal structure
  • 2-story requires interior enclosed stairs
Standard DADU (BL2025-1007)

Max living: 850 SF (lot <10K) / 1,200 SF (lot ≥10K)

Footprint: 850 SF or 50% of principal (up to 2,500 SF)

Review: Codes Department only

Historic Overlay DADU (BL2011-900)

Max living: 700 SF (all lots)

Footprint: 700 SF or 50% of principal (whichever is less)

Review: MHZC + Codes Department

Permit Requirements

Required Documentation

  • Licensed contractor information for each trade
  • Scope of work description
  • Equipment specifications
  • Load calculations (electrical and HVAC)
  • Material list and specifications
  • Recorded restrictive covenant (before building permit)

Inspection Requirements

  • Foundation inspection (before pouring)
  • Rough-in inspection (before drywall)
  • Underground and concealed work inspection
  • Final inspection and testing for each trade
  • 24-hour notice required for inspections
  • Approved permit card posted on site
