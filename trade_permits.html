Permit Requirements

Required Documentation

  • Licensed contractor information for each trade
  • Scope of work description
  • Equipment specifications
  • Load calculations (electrical and HVAC)
  • Material list and specifications
  • Recorded restrictive covenant (before building permit)

Inspection Requirements

  • Foundation inspection (before pouring)
  • Rough-in inspection (before drywall)
  • Underground and concealed work inspection
  • Final inspection and testing for each trade
  • 24-hour notice required for inspections
  • Approved permit card posted on site
