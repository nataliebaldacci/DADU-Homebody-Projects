Requirements for All DADUs

Owner Occupancy

Owner must live in main house OR the DADU.

Restrictive Covenant

Must be recorded before permit issuance.

Height Subordination

Cannot be taller than principal structure.

Size Subordination

Cannot exceed size of main house.

No Short-Term Rentals

Airbnb/VRBO not allowed in RS/RN/RL zones.

Off-Street Parking

One space required for the DADU.
